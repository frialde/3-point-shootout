PRO SHOOTING TIPS & GUIDE

Master the rhythm, beat cold streaks, score big!
🕹️ ARCADE MODE POWER-UPS & BONUSES
ARCADE EXCLUSIVE
🧊
FLOATING ICE BALL (TIME FREEZE)
Tap the floating Ice Ball drifting across the screen to equip it as your next shot. Score it to freeze the Shot Clock for 5 full seconds!
🌟
FLOATING GOLDEN BALL (MAGNET NET)
Tap the floating Golden Ball to equip it. Score it to activate Magnet Net for 5 seconds — creating a magnetic force field around the rim that gently pulls close shots straight into the net!
⏱️
PERFECT RELEASE TIME BONUS (+0.5s)
Starting from Round 4 onwards, every Perfect Green Release adds +0.5 seconds bonus time to your shot clock!
🥶 AVOID COLD STREAKS (3 MISSES IN A ROW)
Missing 3 consecutive shots causes you to turn COLD!
  • When cold, your shooting meter will shake and travel 10% faster.
  • An icy particle ring freezes the rim, making accurate timing much harder.
  • How to Recover:
    • Easy & Normal Difficulty: Score 1 make to break cold status.
    • Hard Difficulty: Score 2 consecutive makes to recover!
    • Once recovered, meter speed reverts back to normal.
🎯 TARGET PERFECT GREEN RELEASES
Release the meter in the exact center of the GREEN ZONE for a 100% guaranteed swish! Perfect releases build hot streak momentum rapidly.
🔥 BUILD HOT STREAKS (3+ MAKES)
Hitting 3 or more consecutive makes ignites Fire Ball Trails and unlocks a shot forgiveness boost on non-perfect releases:
  • Easy & Normal Difficulty: +5% make chance boost on non-perfect shots.
  • Hard Difficulty: +10% make chance boost on non-perfect shots!
💰 MONEY BALLS & STARRY RANGE (2 & 3 PTS)
• Money Balls (2 PTS): The 5th ball on each rack, plus ALL 5 balls on your selected Money Ball rack.
• Starry Range Balls (3 PTS): Deep balls placed 6 feet behind the 3-point line!
⏱️ PACING & SHOT CLOCK
You have 70 seconds to shoot all 27 balls. Establish a rhythm so you don't run out of time on the final rack!
